- **Why:** the burst path appears to break the model’s ability to keep the attack coherent; the extra parallelism does not translate into more valid findings.
- **Learning:** keep **BURST_K closed**. It is not a rescue lever, even when the template itself is valid.

### v32 — 85.410
- **What happened:** the 0.996 margin sweep landed below v20.

- **Learning:** small margin nudges in this band are not enough to beat the baseline by themselves.

### v33 — 87.970
- **What happened:** slow multipost improved a bit over v32 but still missed v20.
- **Why:** the multipost idea adds complexity without a proportional gain in fire rate or candidate count.
- **Learning:** multipost is not a high-confidence win on its own; treat it as secondary to a bigger structural change.

### v30 / v20 parity at 90.135

- **Why:** the SPLIT_CLASSIFY_N=1 change did not unlock extra valid candidates on real Kaggle.
- **Learning:** zero-waste classification is already saturated; do not spend more submissions there.
